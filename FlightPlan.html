From opportunity to healthy portfolio.

The sequence is deliberate. First recognize the likely sales journey. Then qualify the merchant, match the need to the right lane, understand the pricing conversation and decide when support should enter the deal.

Read the opportunity

Use Grades to recognize shorter cycles and complexity, not to label merchants for the sake of labeling them.

Match the solution

Start with the merchant's actual environment and needs before discussing equipment, software or pricing.

Build the portfolio

Keep early wins moving while supported and strategic opportunities develop alongside the rest of your pipeline.
