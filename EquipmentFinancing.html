Equipment cost does not automatically end a good opportunity.

Propelr has 7 equipment financing paths. As a Rookie, you only need to recognize the 4 merchant-facing paths available to you. This is awareness, not a financing deep dive.

Merchant-Facing Options · Available to Rookies

Recommended 01

Merchant Upfront Purchase

The merchant pays the equipment sale price upfront at the time of deployment.

Additional options

02

Merchant Installment Plan (3 or 6 months)

The merchant purchases equipment over a defined installment period. The total purchase price is Propelr's cost plus a 15% total premium.

03

Merchant Lease

The merchant enters into a lease agreement with Propelr's approved leasing partner, Tech Capital Group, LLC (TCG).

04

Merchant Rental

Propelr rents equipment to the merchant under a standard rental agreement, and monthly rental payments are direct debited by Propelr.

Pilot-Facing Options · Not available to Rookies

05

Pilot Residual Offset

Propelr provides the equipment at the Propelr sale price, and the cost is recovered from the Pilot's residuals.

06

Margin Offset Sharing

Propelr provides the equipment with no upfront cash payment. The total equipment cost (Propelr sale price) is shared between Propelr and the Pilot in proportion to their residual split on the applicable MID.

07

100% Propelr-Funded; 25% Split Reduction

Propelr pays the full Propelr sale price with no cost or upfront obligation to the Pilot. In exchange, the Pilot's residual split on that MID is permanently reduced by 25% for the life of the merchant.

Rookie boundary: Options 1–4 are the only financing paths available to Rookie Pilots. Options 5–7 are shown for awareness only and are not part of Rookie solutioning. Visit the Employee Portal and talk with leadership for program terms, documentation and approvals.
