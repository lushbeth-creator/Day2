Equipment cost does not automatically end a good opportunity.

Rookies only need to recognize the 4 merchant-facing paths available to them. This is awareness, not a financing deep dive.

Upfront Purchase

The merchant purchases the equipment upfront.

Installment Purchase

The merchant purchases equipment over an approved 3- or 6-month period.

Merchant Lease

A third-party lease structure is used through the approved leasing partner.

Merchant Rental

The merchant rents equipment from Propelr under the approved rental structure.

Rookie boundary: Options 5–7 are not part of Rookie solutioning. Use the Employee Portal and leadership for program terms, documentation, liabilities and approvals.
