Worth noting

Gas stations have posted a cash price and a credit price side by side for decades. They were the earliest adopters of dual pricing and are still the easiest real-world example when a merchant asks what it looks like.

The pattern to notice: Cash discount and surcharge may both reduce what a merchant pays to accept cards, but they developed through different legal and network paths. That is why their structure, disclosures and rules cannot be treated as interchangeable.
