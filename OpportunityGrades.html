Opportunity Grades

The Grade tells you something about the sales journey.

For a Rookie, the practical question is simple: How quickly can this opportunity move, how much coordination will it require and who should be working beside me? Grades are a sales-cycle framework. They do not replace underwriting risk assessment.

Grade 1

Shorter Cycle

Straightforward payment acceptance and fewer dependencies. These opportunities help build early wins, confidence and portfolio activity.

Grade 2

More Coordination

A supported integration, replacement or operational dependency can add steps. Good opportunities still belong in the pipeline, but may need more support.

Grade 3

Strategic Cycle

Larger, multi-location or integration-heavy opportunities can involve more stakeholders and a longer cycle. Bring leadership alongside you and keep prospecting.
