Follow Up Essentials

Who

Who did you speak with when you visited?

  • Leave a good impression on every employee, you never know who you're talking to
  • Note who you spoke with and any related notes in your tracker
  • Connect with the owner or person you spoke with on social media

Discuss: Why is it important to treat every person you talk to with respect and courtesy?

When

When do you follow up if you don't set the meeting?

  • Follow up within 48 hours
  • Try calling or sending an email with more information
  • If applicable, honor the timeline given to you by the merchant for how they make decisions

Discuss: In your experience, what has been a typical follow-up timeline?

What

What methods or strategies should I use for follow-up?

  • Social media
  • Check to see what promotions the business is running
  • Leave reviews on the business to get your name out there
  • If applicable, come back as a customer
  • Use what you learned in previous conversations to share industry info that will help their business

Discuss: What other strategies have you used to stay connected to a prospect?

How

How do I set my next step to follow up?

  • Lock in permission to revisit by saying, "I will be back in the area on X day, I will stop back in then. Does that work for you?"
  • Show up when you said you would to establish trust
  • Once the prospect is qualified, move them from your tracker into Stratus as a lead
  • Use Stratus to review all set follow-ups for each day

Discuss: What other actions can be taken to ensure you are accountable for follow-ups?
