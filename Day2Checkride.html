Before you move into sales conversations…

A Pilot should be able to demonstrate these behaviors, not just recognize solution names.

Read the sales cycle

Recognize which opportunities are likely to move sooner and which require more coordination.

Qualify before solutioning

Ask enough questions to recommend from the merchant's needs, not from a product list.

Separate pricing conversations

Distinguish processing pricing from surcharge, cash discount and dual pricing.

Protect portfolio health

Use support on complexity without stopping the prospecting and pipeline development around it.
