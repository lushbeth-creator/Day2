A Two-Stop Appointment

Discovery Appointment:

Spend about 30 minutes with the merchant discovering pain points, reservations, additional decision makers and the decision timeline. Leave with 3 months of their merchant processing statements.

Decision Appointment:

Get to yes or no. Maybe is the worst place to be. Ask for the close. Schedule 40 minutes with the merchant to discuss getting started with Propelr.
