New Merchant Scenario

Using the scenario below, plan and prepare for what you would do next for this merchant.

During an initial visit to The Daily Grind Coffee House, you speak with Maya, the shift supervisor working behind the counter. You have a friendly conversation and learn that the business has been open for three weeks.

Maya shares that the team is still learning the new payment system and has experienced some challenges managing tips and pulling daily reports. She explains that the owner, Jordan, makes decisions about payments and technology but is not currently available. She mentions that Jordan is usually at the shop on weekday afternoons.

What should your next steps be to follow up with this merchant and connect with the decision-maker?

Think through who, when, what and how as you plan your approach.
