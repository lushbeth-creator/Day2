Elements of Effective Appointment Setting

The purpose of this section is to help you be prepared to share information about Propelr and how we are different from our competitors. Our goal is to create conversation when talking to a prospect, so we can set an appointment.

What Does an Effective Appointment Setting Conversation Sound Like?

  • Build rapport before you get into business
  • Follow the 80/20 rule: spend 80% of the conversation listening, 20% talking
  • Ask questions with a Propelr solution already in mind
  • Be genuinely curious about how their business runs today
  • Leave with a clear next step to follow up on

What Questions Should I Ask?

"Hi, I'm [Name] with Propelr. We help local businesses with payments and technology. I wanted to introduce myself and learn a little about your business."
  • "How are you currently handling your payment processing, and how happy are you with it?"
  • "What would you change about your current setup if you could?"
  • "Have you looked at your last statement for fees or rate changes?"
  • "Is there a piece of equipment or system you feel like you've outgrown?"
  • "Would you be open to a short meeting to see if there's room to improve on what you have?"

What Should I Do With the Information I Gather?

  • Document it in Stratus right away, while it's still fresh
  • Match what you heard to a Propelr solution, like dual pricing, surcharge or equipment financing, so you walk into the discovery meeting with a direction, not a blank page
  • Confirm who the real decision-maker is, and who else needs to be involved
  • Lock in a specific date and time before you leave, don't let it stay open-ended
  • Send a brief confirmation so the appointment actually sticks
