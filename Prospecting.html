Who should I talk to?

Who should I talk to?

Focus on locally owned small and midsize businesses where the owner or decision-maker is likely to be accessible.

Good prospects include:

  • Restaurants, cafés and bars
  • Retail shops and boutiques
  • Salons, spas and barbershops
  • Auto repair and service businesses
  • Medical, dental and professional offices

Prioritize businesses that accept card payments regularly, could benefit from better technology or service and allow you to build a relationship with the owner.

Step 2

What strategy should you use?

What strategy should you use?

Build a manageable list of businesses within a specific geographic area.

  • Choose one neighborhood, business district or ZIP code
  • Research businesses before visiting
  • Group nearby prospects into an efficient route
  • Visit during slower business hours
  • Start with businesses you already know or frequent
  • Look for opportunities to ask for neighboring-business referrals
  • Record every interaction and next step in Stratus

Begin with a focused list of quality prospects, not random businesses scattered across a large area.

Step 3

What does success look like?

What does success look like?

Set activity goals that you can control.

A successful prospecting block might include:

  • 10 to 15 targeted SMBs identified
  • 5 to 10 businesses visited
  • All activity documented in Stratus

The immediate goal is to build a healthy pipeline and secure discovery appointments, not close every merchant during the first visit.

Step 4

What are the best ways to start the conversation?

What are the best ways to start the conversation?

Begin with places and connections closest to you.

  • Businesses you already visit
  • Local restaurants and retailers
  • Friends or family members who own businesses
  • Businesses within your neighborhood
  • Chamber of Commerce or networking directories
  • Google Maps searches by business category
  • Newly opened or "coming soon" businesses
  • Local Facebook and community groups

Choose one SMB category or geographic area, build your first prospect list and create a route before heading out.
